# Method Storyteller 0.3.0

Television scripts laid out in acts, and one Mac app that runs on both Apple silicon and Intel.

## Write for television

- **New Act and End of Act lines.** TEASER, COLD OPEN, ACT ONE through ACT FIVE and TAG, in
  capitals and centred, each act starting on a new page. Pick them from the element picker
  (B for New Act, E for End of Act) or from the Format menu.
- **File > New TV Script** lays out a one-hour or half-hour script in acts, ready to write.
  It asks which, and remembers your choice.
- **Acts in the scene navigator,** each above its scenes. A click on an act goes into its
  first scene.
- **Go To an act** (⌘⌥G, then Act): by its number or its name, such as 2, TAG or ACT TWO.
- **Act lines travel with the script** in `.fdx`, Open Screenplay Format and Fountain.

## On every Mac

- **One download for every Mac.** The app now runs natively on Intel Macs as well as Apple
  silicon, and updates itself on both.

## Not there yet

- Scripts with act lines saved by this version show those lines as action in 0.2.0. Update
  before opening them there.
- An End of Act line doesn't yet change when you rename its act.
- No revision mode, printing or side-by-side editing of dual dialogue yet.
- On Windows and Linux, download each new version from the website.
