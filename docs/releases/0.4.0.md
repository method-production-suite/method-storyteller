# Method Storyteller 0.4.0

Bold, italic and underline in the editor, and television scripts finished: multi-camera
sitcoms, and End of Act lines that look after themselves.

## Style your words

- **Bold, italic and underline.** Format > Bold (⌘B), Italic (⌘I) and Underline (⌘U) style
  the words you select, or the word the caret is in. Press it again to take the style off.
- **The editor shows the styles** in the script's own typeface. The markers that save them
  in the file stay faint beside the words.

## Write for television

- **A multi-camera half-hour** in File > New TV Script: Cold Open, Acts One and Two, and Tag.
  Scenes are lettered A, B, C, and a new scene takes its letter. Each scene starts on a new page
  with its speaking characters listed under the heading. Dialogue is double-spaced and action is
  in capitals.
- **End of Act lines follow their acts.** Rename ACT TWO and END OF ACT TWO changes with it.
  Name a new act and its End of Act line appears. A line of your own, such as END OF SHOW,
  stays as you wrote it.
- **Scripts that set their own spacing, page breaks or capitals** now look that way on the
  page and in the PDF, so a multi-camera `.fdx` opens as it was written.

## Safer downloads

- **The checksum list is signed** (`SHA256SUMS.minisig`), so you can check that every
  download came from us.

## Not there yet

- Typing into a part of the script set to capitals stores capitals, not your own casing.
- No revision mode, printing or side-by-side editing of dual dialogue yet.
- On Windows and Linux, download each new version from the website.
