# Method Storyteller 0.2.0

The first release: a screenwriting app that writes the way scripts are written, lays out pages
the way productions print them, and opens and saves the files a script travels in.

## Write the way scripts are written

- **Enter and Tab move between elements.** Scene heading, action, character, parenthetical,
  dialogue and transition follow each other as you type; ⌘1 to ⌘7 pick one directly, and
  Enter on an empty line opens the element picker.
- **Autocomplete for characters, locations and times of day,** a guess at who speaks next,
  automatic (CONT'D), and smart quotes.
- **Type INT. or EXT. and the line becomes a scene heading,** even when you paste a whole
  heading into an empty line.
- **Spell check as you type.** Misspelled words get a thin red underline; Edit > Spelling…
  (⌘;) steps through them with suggestions, Change, Ignore and Learn. Character names and
  screenplay terms are never flagged.
- **Cut, Copy, Paste and Select All** in the Edit menu, as well as on the usual keys.

## Find your way round a script

- **The scene navigator** lists every scene with its number, page and length in eighths. A
  click takes you straight into the scene's action, ready to type.
- **Go To a scene or a page** (⌘⌥G), **find and replace** with one Undo for Replace All, and
  **rename a character** everywhere at once.
- **Page view and draft view,** title page, scene numbers on both margins, and zoom.

## Pages a production expects

- **Pages break where a production expects them:** Courier Prime 12 pt, standard margins,
  (MORE) and (CONT'D) at page breaks, and scene pages and lengths that match real production
  scripts.
- **Export PDF** with selectable text and a bookmark for each scene.

## Open and save the files a script travels in

- **Open Screenplay Format (`.osf`) is the standard for new scripts.** Fountain stays fully
  supported: open, save, and File > Save New Scripts as Fountain.
- **`.fdx` files open and save,** keeping every part of the file the app does not use. Script
  notes, revisions, locked pages, element settings and page layout, and the words spell check
  ignores carry over between `.fdx` and Open Screenplay Format, both ways.
- **Fountain's boneyard (`/* … */`) is kept,** and saving to a format says what it will not
  keep.
- **On a Mac, double-click a script in the Finder** to open it here; `.osf`, `.fountain` and
  `.fdx` files show their own document icons.
- **The first time you open the app,** it shows the sample script, Field Station, as an
  unsaved copy to look round. Help > Open Sample Script brings it back.

## Not there yet

- No revision mode, printing or side-by-side editing of dual dialogue yet.
- Automatic updates work on Apple silicon Macs; on Windows and Linux, download the next
  version from the website.
- The spelling dictionary is US English.
