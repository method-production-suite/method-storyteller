# Method Storyteller 0.7.0

Locked pages, production reports and an export for scheduling, with selections that cross
paragraphs and a quicker editor for long scripts.

## Pages that stay put

- **Production > Lock Pages** keeps each page's number from then on. What no longer fits flows
  onto A-pages (`12A`), a page that lost text stays short, and pages whose text fits on one
  print as `7-8`. **Unlock All Pages** numbers them straight through again.
- **Production > Lock A-Pages** locks the A-pages there are now, so later pages letter around
  them (`12AA` before a locked `12A`). **Unlock A-Pages** takes the newest of those locks away.
- **Locks travel with the script.** Pages locked in an `.osf` file keep their numbers here, and
  locked pages saved as `.fdx` stay locked when opened again.
- **Revision mode marks whole paragraphs.** A new paragraph is marked whole, whether Enter
  splits it off or a paste makes it, and joining two paragraphs leaves a mark where they met.
  Revision mode is kept in the script, on or off.

## Reports and scheduling

- **Production > Reports…** shows the scene, location, character and cast reports in one
  window with their totals. Export each as CSV, or print it as a PDF table.
- **File > Export for Method Schedule…** writes the scenes with this app's own pages and
  lengths, so the schedule starts from the real numbers, not estimates.
- **File > Export Plain Text…** writes the script as it reads on the page, with no markup.

## Writing and moving around

- **Selections cross paragraphs.** Drag, Shift-click or Shift and the arrows past a
  paragraph's edge. Cut and Copy keep every element's type, and ⌘A selects the whole script.
- **Drag a scene in the navigator** to move it, with everything in it, as one Undo.
- **Bookmarks.** Edit > Add Bookmark… names the paragraph you are in, and Go To lists them.
- **View > Show Outline** shows or hides sections and synopses on screen.
- **Autocomplete lists are kept in the script.** Production > Autocomplete Lists… shows and
  edits characters, extensions, locations, times and transitions.
- **File > Open reads `.highland` files.**
- **Long scripts are quicker.** Paragraphs off screen are not laid out each frame, and page
  breaks and scene lengths on screen update half a second after you stop typing. Saving,
  printing and reports always use the pages as they are.

## Fixed

- **A new revision set is named with today's date where you are.** In the evening in the
  Americas it could carry tomorrow's date.
- **Report CSV files are safe to open in a spreadsheet.** A cell that starts like a formula is
  never run as one.

## Not there yet

- Pages locked in an `.fdx` file are kept and saved back, but print as unlocked until their
  positions are read reliably.
- Scene CONTINUED at page breaks is not offered yet.
- On Windows and Linux, download each new version from the website.
